    A brief guide to using AI at UCL

    Here are some pointers for members of the department thinking about using AI, in the sense of generative AI systems powered by deep learning models. This page will be updated, but may contain obsolete information as these technologies are changing rapidly, as are Microsoft’s product names.

    The Generative AI Hub is a central UCL resource containing information about GenAI with a focus on teaching, learning and assessment. It includes links to other sources of information and to details of services offered by UCL, and limited discussion of some of the ethical and practical concerns about the training and application of these models.

    What does UCL provide?

    Information and training

    In addition to the information on the Generative AI Hub, the Generative AI Training site gives details of various training sessions. There are recordings available, and the slides are useful in themselves:

    • GenAI Fundamentals 1: the bare essentials
    • GenAI Fundamentals 2: using GenAI responsibly including discussion of bias, hallucination, sycophancy, data security, sustainability, the digital divide, exploitation of workers, and misappropriation of intellectual property
    • GenAI Fundamentals 3: navigating UCL’s GenAI landscape including information about UCL’s policies on GenAI, especially in assessment

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

    This is the only general-purpose AI service that is available to all UCL staff and students without additional cost. It is part of the Microsoft 365 Copilot suite, but includes only a chat interface, without any integration with other Microsoft products. Apart from text, it can deal with images, audio and video files, and various data and document types. It has no direct access to your data, but you can upload files or browse to specific files in the Microsoft Cloud (OneDrive, SharePoint, or Teams), and download files created by the service.

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is recommended by UCL because if you sign in with your UCL account your session benefits from Enterprise Data Protection, meaning Microsoft offers additional security and undertakes not to use your data to train its models. You can log in using your UCL credentials at M365 Copilot or by selecting the Microsoft 365 Copilot icon from the app launcher (grid icon) in the top-left corner at outlook.com. Make sure you see a green shield icon at the top right of the page, which when hovered over says Enterprise data protection applies to this chat.

    This data protection does not apply to the consumer version of Microsoft Copilot, currently called simply Copilot by Microsoft, which is available for free without a login.

    UCL advises (at Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat) that “Although your data is more secure when using Copilot Chat with your UCL credentials, we still advise that you don’t enter personal, or special category data into the tool. In particular, staff should not upload students’ work into the tool.”

    Microsoft 365 Copilot

    The full Microsoft 365 Copilot suite is integrated with other Microsoft products and can use data to which you as a user have access within the Microsoft cloud. It requires an additional licence costing £15 per user per month (as of May 2026) with a one-month free trial available.

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Agents

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Agents are customized chat interfaces that you can create for specific tasks. Limited functionality is available through the Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat interface, with additional features available if you have a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence.

    GitHub Copilot

    The rules have changed several times, but you may be able to get free access to GitHub Copilot Pro by becoming a verified teacher on GitHub Education.

    Studiosity

    Studiosity is a more specific service to “support students with their academic writing and referencing skills” by automatically generating feedback on writing and referencing. It is available without charge to students and staff.

    What does the Department provide?

    Currently the Department of Physics and Astronomy does not have any central subscriptions to any GenAI services. It is up to groups and individuals to choose which, if any, to use, and to find any funding required.

    What will UCL provide in the future?

    A joint project called AI@UCL is being undertaken by ISD and ARC with the aim of providing a “safe umbrella for valuable use cases”. It is intended to provide access to both commercial and locally hosted models via predefined services (in the AI@UCL Shop) and to underpin development (in the AI@UCL Foundry).

    As of May 2026, there are trials underway with invited individuals. Services should be offered to successively wider audiences until they are made generally available at the start of the 2026-27 academic year.

    Using other services

    If the services offered by UCL do not meet your requirements, there is no general rule against paying for subscriptions to other providers such as Anthropic (for Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google (Gemini) or using free services. However, it is as always the responsibility of the budget holder to ensure they are getting value for money, and complying with any conditions set by the funder.

    Comparison of models and tools

    It is hard to provide a useful comparison because services are evolving rapidly, but also because providers are not transparent about what they are offering. As of early 2026, Microsoft 365 Copilot uses the GPT-5 family of models from OpenAI. It uses “dynamic routing” to select a specific model to handle each query.

    Claude, from Anthropic, is favoured by many users especially because of its Claude Code application, a command-line tool that runs locally on your machine and can interact directly with your files and installed applications such as Python or Mathematica. It offers a choice of models from Anthropic, including (as of 15th May 2026) Sonnet 4.6, Opus 4.7, and Haiku 4.5. It can also be used with other models, including ones you can host on your own machine, if they provide the required Anthropic API.
